# Rental Applications Overview

The HomeHunt applications module transitions property discovery and viewings into structured rental applications, applicant reviews, and landlord decisions.

## Objectives

- **Seeker Empowerment**: Allow seekers to submit rental applications securely, track status updates, and supply additional information or documents requested by landlords.
- **Provider Streamlining**: Enable landlords and property managers to receive completed profiles, review sensitive documents securely, request additional information, shortlist applicants, and log final rental decisions.
- **Platform Integrity**: Provide a secure, transactional, and auditable bridge from discovery to tenancy without exposing sensitive documents or personal data to unauthorized parties.

## Conversion Funnel

The platform measures user conversions along the following key milestones:

1. **Listing View**: Seeker discovers property details.
2. **Viewing Completed**: Seeker attends a physical showing of the property.
3. **Application Started**: Seeker initializes a draft application.
4. **Application Submitted**: Seeker completes all profile details and checklist documents, then submits.
5. **Shortlisted**: Seeker is moved to a shortlist by the provider.
6. **Approved**: Seeker is approved for the next stage (handoff to Phase 8).

## Business Intelligence Metrics

To understand dropout rates and improve platform performance, HomeHunt tracks aggregate, anonymized metrics:
- Application start-to-submission completion rates.
- Time-to-first-review and time-to-decision SLAs.
- Document rejection rates to adjust form layout or upload instructions.
- Common rejection reasons to filter out low-intent listing traffic.
