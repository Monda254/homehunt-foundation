# Legal Readiness & Kenyan Regulatory Compliance Report

## Compliance Matrix

| Regulation                                           | Scope / Requirement                                               | Platform Enforcement                                                                                              |
| :--------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Kenya Data Protection Act (2019)**                 | Lawful processing of tenant PII, national ID, and financial data. | Explicit user consent during registration, encrypted storage, and right-to-be-forgotten export/deletion handlers. |
| **Landlord and Tenant Act (Cap 301)**                | Regulation of tenancy terms and rent obligations.                 | Standardized digital lease agreements with audit logs of tenant and landlord timestamps.                          |
| **National Environment Management Authority (NEMA)** | Noise & property habitability disclosures.                        | Verification checklists for listed rental units prior to publication.                                             |

## Consumer Protection & Trust Language

All public UI strings have been audited to avoid misleading claims. HomeHunt presents defensible trust descriptions ("Verification-backed housing platform") rather than absolute guarantees.
